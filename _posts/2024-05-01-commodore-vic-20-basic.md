---
title: Commodore VIC-20 BASIC
code: PRINT 0.1 + 0.2
result: " .3"
---

Commodore VIC-20 BASIC (Commodore BASIC V2) uses 40-bit floating point numbers (1-byte exponent and 4-byte mantissa). When printing floating-point values, BASIC V2 rounds numbers to 9 significant digits and formats positive numbers with a leading space while omitting leading zeros before the decimal point.
